# 03 · Age-based needs matrix

**Evidence labels:** **[E]** = consistent with public guidance (WHO, CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early" 2022, AAP / HealthyChildren.org, Vietnam's national immunisation schedule). **[H]** = product hypothesis to validate with families. Ages are typical, never expectations: children vary widely, and families differ in routines, caregivers and culture. Nothing here assumes which parent does what.

## 2. The 3–5 problems Child Life can realistically solve

### CH01 · 0–12 months
1. **"What happened and when?"** Logging and handover across caregivers. *High-frequency.* Built.
2. **"Is this normal?"** Calm, sourced guidance by age, and knowing when to call a doctor. *Occasional, urgent.* Built (content pending review).
3. **"Don't let me miss it"** Vaccines, reminders. *Milestone-driven.* Built.
4. **"Keep the firsts"** Photos, monthly keepsakes. *Emotional, monthly.* Built.

### CH02 · 1–3 years
1. **Keep the firsts going**: first steps, words, birthdays, without daily effort. *Emotional, milestone-driven.* Partly built (occasions stop at y1).
2. **Light routines**: sleep and diaper/potty, a helper's handover. *Daily, lighter.* Built (potty not built).
3. **Growth and boosters**. *Occasional, record-keeping.* Built.
4. **Understanding development** to 36 months. *Occasional.* Built for milestones, content partial.

### CH03 · 3–6 years
1. **A living memory book**: photos sorted by age, yearly stories, sharing with grandparents. *Weekly, emotional.* Infrastructure built; prompts missing.
2. **Family logistics**: costs (school, fees), reminders, who picks up. *Weekly.* Built (Chi tiêu, Nhắc nhở, Cùng Chăm Con generic tasks).
3. **Health record**: boosters to 6 y, growth to 5 y, doctor-visit prep. *Occasional.* Built.
4. **Development and readiness questions**. *Occasional.* Not built (content); **DEFER**.

### CH04 · 6–12 years
1. **Preserving childhood**: yearly look-back, Then & Now, achievements as memories. *Event-driven, highly emotional.* Infrastructure built.
2. **Records that last**: vaccines, growth history, documents. *Rare but high value.* Partly built (no documents).
3. **Family coordination**: reminders and spending. *Weekly.* Built.

## 3. Need types

| Need type | CH01 | CH02 | CH03 | CH04 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High-frequency | Care logs, timers | Sleep log, photos | Photos | — |
| Occasional | Guidance, Lumi questions | Milestones, food | Expenses, reminders | Expenses, reminders |
| Milestone-driven | Monthly, 100 days, first birthday | Birthdays, first words/steps | Birthdays, school start | Birthdays, school year, achievements |
| Long-term records | Vaccines, growth | Vaccines, growth | Vaccines, growth, visit prep | Vaccines, growth history |
| Emotional engagement | Monthly cards, week story | Birthday video, firsts | Year story, sharing with relatives | Year look-back, Then & Now |

## 4. What this means for the product

- The **daily** reason to open the app fades after year one for most families [H]. The **yearly and event-driven** reasons (birthdays, look-backs, records) grow. The product must stop measuring older-child value by daily opens.
- Families with older children need **less input, more output**: the app should turn what they already store (photos, records) into meaning, not ask for more logging.
- **Validate first** ([12 §5](12-prioritized-roadmap.md)): frequency by band, whether CH03/CH04 families use Chi tiêu and Nhắc nhở, whether they would bring older photos in.
