## Storage, queries and privacy

- Private: `productAnalyticsFacts`, `productAnalyticsAccounts`, `productAnalyticsDeletions`. Existing deny-by-default Rules reject direct client reads/writes, including the user's own analytics documents.
- `staffProductAnalytics` requires `analytics.view`; Cost additionally requires `premium.view`. Export requires `analytics.export` and writes the existing staff audit record. Staff accounts are excluded from product cohorts.
- Aggregate-only `productAnalyticsQueries`: five-minute cache, two-day expiry. One worker builds five product sections together; Cost is separate. Portal render does not scan operational baby/family data.
- **Implementation refinement to PA-T03:** bounded asynchronous materialization over private facts, rather than the full incremental daily/sharded pipeline. Pages of 500, maximum 200,000 rows per scanned source, concurrency one/max two worker instances, maximum 20 new queries per staff member per five minutes. Bound violations return unavailable, never truncated results. Section switches reuse snapshots. Load/SLO acceptance remains pending measured evidence.
- Minimum cohort: 20 users/families. Unknown, suppressed, unsupported, immature and observed zero are distinct. Small breakdown slices are withheld; hidden denominators are not returned. This is not differential privacy; overlapping/custom-date query policy still needs PA-02/PA-05 review.
- Account/baby deletion purges private facts and deletes/redacts context profiles, establishes replay fences and increments a privacy epoch. No snapshot can publish or serve while any purge is active. Duplicate concurrent purge deliveries cannot decrement the barrier twice. Old snapshots cannot bypass epoch checks.
- Retention prepared in code: account/non-ENG facts 400 days; ENG eligibility joins through their window plus 30 days, other ENG stages 30 days after the stage; deletion fences 401 days; snapshot/query budgets two days. Daily cleanup is bounded to 3,000 expired documents per collection. TTL declarations are prepared in `firestore.indexes.json` as a second mechanism, **not applied to an environment**.
- **PA-05 review before activation:** one fact serves as both receipt and identity contribution; no separate <=120-day transient observation receipt store exists. Its 400-day private observation retention needs explicit review against the earlier receipt/fact distinction. Shorter approved retention must make unsupported windows unavailable. ENG ranges older than the available 30-day joins remain unavailable until sealed historical aggregates exist.

## Activation, rollback and release gates

`product_analytics` ships **off**, uses existing flag mirroring and appears under Product measurement in Portal feature controls. Off stops new foundation collection; domain actions and legacy counters continue. Historical aggregates remain permission-controlled. The flag does not change the existing AI usage observer or send notifications.

After implementation/privacy approval, an approved dev deployment should publish backend and Portal together, apply prepared indexes/TTL and enable collection through the existing audited flag workflow. Do not seed fake baselines or relabel legacy metrics as V2. Compare real source receipts with Portal for registration, foreground without push permission, review/upload-ready, Care/Grow save, Story/render/file/share and family viewing. Verify offline restart, permission revocation, two devices/one user, two caregivers/one family, account/baby deletion and disabled collection. Repeat on **real iOS and Android devices**, recording app/backend revisions. These live dev/device steps have **not** run in this session. Device discovery sees a physical iPhone; no physical Android was listed.

Before production: approve retention/query/Chapter decisions; complete upstream funnel/ENG measurement dependencies; pass automated, privacy, real-device and measured-load gates; demonstrate kill switch/deletion; approve release/config separately. Production Launch with Measurement releases the complete approved V1 together. Small populations remain descriptive.

Rollback through the approved workflow: disable foundation intake, retain working domain actions/legacy APIs, stop the V2 query worker or roll back Portal/backend together if needed. Do not silently substitute legacy definitions, drop private collections or run destructive backfills.
