## Cohort entry, channel eligibility và attribution

- `sourceEligible`: source/time/media/role đủ để opportunity hữu ích; ghi cohort **trước** OS permission, consent/cap/fatigue, recipient-channel decision hoặc experiment assignment. No source permission/role thì assessed-excluded, không eligible. Entry frozen per logical O; conditions chưa biết = unknown, không zero.
- `pushEligible`: thêm approved consent/category settings, permission/platform capability, flags, fatigue/budget/quiet-hours/device lease và expiry; không dùng token presence để định nghĩa eligible family population.
- Một O/recipient/window có một logical identity; family campaign dedup qua coParents, baby/device/source generation. Retry/locale/token/policy-version change không tạo cohort mới. Distinct U/F và opportunities hiển thị riêng, không cộng caregivers thành gia đình.
- `completed in eligible cohort` bao gồm direct/manual/in-app completion, có thể không tap notification. `explicitly attributed completion` cần liên kết intent→approved batch/job/occurrence/view, one outcome attributed≤1 intent; không đoán nhân quả chỉ vì thời điểm gần nhau.

## Lifecycle reporting contract

| Stage                                                        | Authority                                                                     | Denominator/interpretation                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Assessed/excluded/unknown                                    | Source policy evaluation                                                      | Scope coverage; excluded không tự added vào eligible count                            |
| Eligible                                                     | Frozen source/role/time receipt                                               | Primary denominator distinct O; eligible users/families separate                      |
| In-app only/delay/group/suppress/cancel/expire/push eligible | Deterministic decision+reason                                                 | Report decision histories vs terminal counts separately; retries không eligible again |
| Scheduled                                                    | Existing local schedule/reservation ack                                       | Not shown/fired/delivered                                                             |
| Dispatch attempted                                           | Adapter attempt receipt                                                       | Attempts and distinct intents both; no token-count unique users                       |
| Provider accepted                                            | Push service accepted                                                         | Proxy handoff only; không device display rate                                         |
| Dispatched local                                             | Trusted native firing handoff nếu có                                          | Registration of future local schedule không là firing; unavailable→unknown            |
| Presentation observed                                        | Trusted actual receipt/QA observation clearly scoped                          | Coverage metric, không extrapolate một test device ra production delivery rate        |
| Opened                                                       | Tap callback + authorized resolver result; suggestion click has surface riêng | Không guarantee destination content visible hoặc value complete                       |
| Completed                                                    | Correct domain outcome + explicit join                                        | Completed/eligible; direct/manual branch included overall, attribution branch riêng   |

Provider accepted nhưng không presentation evidence = delivery unknown. Không báo “CTR theo delivered” thiếu delivered denominator. Có thể báo opened/provider-accepted **proxy**, channel/window/coverage rõ; local scheduled denominator riêng, không gộp. Dismiss chỉ explicit UI/known OS callback; “ignored” chỉ trusted presentation + mature response window + reconciled telemetry, không non-open accepted-only.

## Bốn funnels bắt buộc

1. **Photos:** newly discovered readable P/E → review visible → approved → server cloud ready; show skipped/rejected/resolved-all-skip, queued/processing/unreadable/source-lost separately. Capture date không là cohort date. Eight-photo case: five already ready leaves three pending; three approved but two ready means2/3 ready, không3/3.
2. **Reminders:** eligible recipient-O → scheduled/adapter attempt/accepted or unknown → opened → completed. Primary rates stage/eligible cohort, transition rates additional clearly labeled. Completion direct không bị loại vì không notification open.
3. **Memory Story video:** qualified ready media-set → creator started → render requested → rendered → **filed/ready** → optional observed share result. Text Story draft→saved có branch riêng; share sheet/link created không confirmed external share/read.
4. **Milestone:** qualified family-occasion → template actually opened → video filed ready; job/render stages hỗ trợ diagnosis. Current vs retrospective cohorts và windows riêng; completed keepsake trước eligibility suppress, không giả new conversion.

Detailed formulas và units trong [dictionary](02-metric-dictionary.md), canonical events trong [tracking](03-event-tracking-plan.md). Không tính conversion bằng tổng `autoSaveApproved/autoSaveSuggested` hoặc `keepsakeFiled/qualifiedFamilies` legacy. Time-to-stage phân tích trên observed completions phải kèm conversion/coverage, không giấu non-completers.

## Controls, comparison và small population

Không separate pilot hoặc holdout bắt buộc. Nếu sau launch có approved concurrent experiment, assignment household-level stable theo experiment version, baseline eligibility trước assignment; control giữ in-app tương đương và không proactive increment đang thử. Configured Care/transactional ready/messages/user preferences không randomize. Schema hỗ trợ intent-to-treat, exposure, attrition, sample/maturity/guardrails; không tự bật experiment để có dashboard.

No assignment → no control/treatment comparison, không dùng opt-out/permission-denied làm control tương đương. Before/after/late-vs-early/locale/platform comparisons chỉ descriptive; khác cohorts và concurrent changes phải visible. Nếu N nhỏ hoặc cell bị suppress, hiển thị insufficient data, không fabricated lift/p-value/statistical significance. Growth targets TBD sau actual baseline. Correctness/security/cap tests vẫn là release gates ngay, không chờ large N.

## Guardrails và Portal actions

- **Rights/privacy:** stale-UID/revoked content/open attempts, payload rejection; confirmed leak blocks release hoặc approved kill switch, không raw child details để debug.
- **Orchestration:** duplicate logical push, rolling cap violations, quiet-hours/expiry attempts, cancel success vs unknown, legacy/new producer collision; invariant failure không sửa bằng tăng retry volume.
- **Fatigue/preferences:** explicit opt-out/category, dismiss/snooze, reduced/paused, post-completion quiet; denied/unknown delivery không strike. Report affected eligible users, không chỉ button counts.
- **Source/value:** partial upload/render/filed, stale source, missing batch/job attribution, card vs video vs Story semantics. Missing join không fake no-conversion.
- **Data:** coverage by channel/platform/client version, provisional windows, late corrections, missing eligible/assignment, source watermark; suggested check tới aggregate reason breakdown, không người cụ thể.

V1 acceptance: synthetic known-denominator/replay/offline/multi-caregiver fixtures reconcile; real iOS/Android lifecycle/permission/timezone/device tests + privacy validation; feature/dispatch/category kill switches tested and Care/transactional regressions absent. Automated/UI fixtures không thay physical notification delivery gate. Production configuration/deployment vẫn cần implementation và release approval.
