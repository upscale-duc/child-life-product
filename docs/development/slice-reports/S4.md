# S4 — Care logging core & offline (in progress)

**Status:** care logging works end to end on iOS and Android against the dev backend code on the local emulators. The backend (Rules, indexes, trigger and purge job) is deployed to `child-life-dev`.

What works:

- logging bottle, breastfeed, pumping, diaper and growth;
- Today's tiles and "Đã ghi hôm nay" totals;
- record detail with edit, soft delete and 5-second undo;
- a save made offline shows "Chờ đồng bộ", stays on the device and syncs when the network returns;
- the server keeps day summaries and each member's "đã ghi N lần".

**Not done:**

- Sleep logging and every timer ("Bấm giờ", sleep timer, overlap) are S5. The sleep tile and "Ngủ" say so.
- History (the "Lịch sử" link, "Xem tất cả") is S6. The first-run coachmarks 18a–18c come with S5.
- ReminderPeek is S15. "Nhắn Mẹ" waits for messages (S10, Q-007).
- Pump per-side amounts (G-062) and care notes (G-020) are not designed, so they are not built.

Checklist rows are ◐, never ☑.

## Built

### Backend (deployed to dev)

- **Contracts** `packages/contracts/src/care.ts`: record shapes and ranges, day bounds and day summaries in the baby's zone.
- **Rules** for `babies/{b}/records`:
  - parents and caregivers log;
  - relatives read only with "Chăm sóc hằng ngày" (D-018);
  - the author snapshot must match the writer's membership;
  - no future times (5 minutes of clock skew allowed);
  - type and author are immutable;
  - deletes are soft, with undo; hard deletes are refused.
- **Trigger** `onCareRecordWrite`: recomputes `days/{date}` for every local day a write touches (idempotent) and the member's `recordCount`.
- **Scheduled** `purgeCareBin`: deletes records 30 days after they were soft-deleted.

### App (`features/care`, `features/today`)

- **Today (19):**
  - tiles per tracker show value · how long ago · who logged it, or "Chờ đồng bộ" while pending;
  - after a save a tile glows (not in Night care) and shows ✓;
  - long-press opens the last record;
  - "Đã ghi hôm nay" totals and the 24 h strip;
  - relatives with daily care see it read-only.
- **Logging:**
  - quick log 19a;
  - 22 / 23 feed sheet: remembers the type and milk; suggests the other breast side;
  - 23a pumping;
  - 24 diaper: remembers the last kind;
  - 28 growth: shows the change since the last measurement;
  - times come from a wheel and are never in the future.
- **Record detail:** 21 with edit and delete; the caregiver's view-only 12e.
- **Offline:**
  - every write goes straight to the Firestore cache;
  - 9a banner, 9b pending list and "Thử lại ngay";
  - 14f shows "đã ghi N lần".
- **Strings:** vi + en, 111 new ids (`tool/s4_strings.py`).

- Toasts can be shown after their sheet has closed (`ClToaster`).
