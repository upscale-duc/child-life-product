**Status:** albums, Yêu thích, Thương and "Nhắn ba mẹ" work in the app against the dev backend code on the local emulators. The relatives' view of Kỷ niệm is filtered on the server by audience. The backend is deployed to `child-life-dev`: Functions, Firestore Rules and indexes. The new callables are invokable by app users.

  - iOS needs APNs, which needs an Apple team (as for S8).
  - Android delivery has not been tried on a device yet.
  - Under the emulators, notices are only marked delivered.
- **10e9 "Tải album về máy" in the background (SP-2):**
  - Saving into a same-named Photos album happens while the app is open.
  - With "Chỉ tải video qua Wi-Fi", videos are skipped off Wi-Fi with a note; they are not queued for later.
- **G-022 "Đã xoá gần đây" screen.** Restore exists as the undo of a delete.
- **11i story reader and "Kể thành chuyện"** (S12). The album shows the entry, disabled.

Checklist rows are ◐, never ☑.

## Decisions

These adopt the business-map proposals:

- **D-034** (Q-007): "Nhắn ba mẹ" is one-way. Relatives and caregivers write; the parents read everything in "Lời nhắn"; a sender sees only their own messages. There is no reply thread.
- **D-035** (Q-014): relatives cannot hide items for themselves.
- **D-036** (Q-015): relatives may download the photos and videos they can see.

## Built

### Backend (deployed to dev)

- **Contract:** `packages/contracts/src/albums.ts` covers album ids, create, add / remove, update (rename, cover, sort, audience) and delete. Compose takes `albumIds` (10h5).
- **Album callables** (`firebase/functions/src/albums.ts`):
  - `createMemoryAlbum`, `addToMemoryAlbum`, `removeFromMemoryAlbum`, `updateMemoryAlbum`, `deleteMemoryAlbum`;
  - parents only;
  - counts and cover kept on the album.
- **Effective audience (D-002, P-035):**
  - An item is Gia đình if its own audience is, or if any Gia đình album holds it (`recomputeEffective`).
  - Setting an album to Chỉ ba mẹ moves its items to Chỉ ba mẹ, except items that another Gia đình album still holds. The count comes back for the "vẫn được gia đình thấy" note.
  - Widening sends one generic notice (`familyNotice`).
- **Deleting an album** keeps the memories and recomputes their audience.
- **Social** (`firebase/functions/src/social.ts`):
  - `onLoveWrite` keeps `loveCount` on media, memories and albums;
  - `onParentMessage` turns a message into a notice for the other Cùng nuôi (D-034);
  - `deliverFamilyNotice` sends generic copy in the recipient's language, never a photo, caption or the baby's name (R-116, R-120), and prunes rejected tokens. Message text is never logged.
- **Rules:**
  - Albums and their items: relatives read Gia đình albums only.
  - Favourites are private to their owner (R-107).
  - Reactions: written by the reactor; read by parents, or by the reactor for their own.
  - Messages: written by members except parents; read by parents, or by the sender for their own.
  - `users/{uid}/devices` belongs to its owner.
