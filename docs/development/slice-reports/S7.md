# S7 — Reminders (in progress)

**Status:**

- Reminders (8a–8e, 12f) and the ReminderPeek on Today work end to end on iOS and Android against the dev backend code on the local emulators.
- Local notifications are scheduled only on the device that holds the reminder (R-091). The server never pushes them.
- The reminder Rules are deployed to `child-life-dev`. There are no new Functions.

**Not done:**

- On a device, these are covered only by Rules and widget tests so far:
  - an overdue reminder;
  - a change of the baby's time zone;
  - moving a notification to another device (D-029).
- The iOS simulator cannot pre-grant notifications. On iOS the end-to-end test therefore answers 8c with "Để sau" and checks 8d; the pending notification itself is checked on Android.
- Android uses inexact while-idle alarms, so a notification can arrive a few minutes late. Exact alarms would need a permission the user must grant.

Checklist rows are ◐, never ☑.

## Decisions (proposals adopted, recorded in the business map)

- **D-029** (Q-005): any member who sees a reminder can move its notification to their own device from the "Thông báo" row (one device at a time). There is still never a push to all devices.
- **D-030** (Q-018):
  - Cùng nuôi can delete a reminder: "Xoá nhắc việc" in the edit form, with a confirmation.
  - Tapping the check of a done reminder undoes it. Any Cùng nuôi can undo; a caregiver can undo only what they marked.
- **D-031** (G-061):
  - "Báo trước" options: Đúng giờ · 10 phút · 30 phút · 1 giờ · 2 giờ · 1 ngày (default 1 giờ).
  - Date and time use a wheel in the baby's time zone, in 5-minute steps, never in the past.
  - Any Cùng nuôi edits any reminder.
- **D-032:** overdue reminders only turn red in the app: one notification at due time minus "Báo trước", no repeats. The reminder keeps its instant when the baby's time zone changes.

## Built

### Backend

- **Data:** `babies/{b}/reminders/{rm_…}` holds title, due instant, lead time, `notifyDevice` (install id, owner uid, model label, owner name), creator snapshot, and done / doneBy / doneAuthor. The contract and tests are in `packages/contracts/src/reminders.ts`.
- **Rules:**
  - Cùng nuôi create, edit and delete.
  - Caregivers view and mark done.
  - Relatives and removed members see nothing (D-018).
  - `notifyDevice` can only name the writer's own device.
  - The due time cannot be set in the past, but a past-due reminder can still be renamed.
  - Done and undo are recorded in the writer's own name.

### App

- **8a list:**
  - groups Quá hạn / Sắp tới / Đã xong, with "Ba tạo" and "Ba đánh dấu xong · 6 thg 10";
  - the ring marks done with "Hoàn tác";
  - info line and "+ Nhắc việc mới".
- **12f:** caregivers see the role note and no create button.
- **8b sheet:**
  - title, Ngày giờ (wheel in the baby's zone), Báo trước, footnote "Theo giờ của Teddy (GMT+7)…";
  - in edit mode: "Sửa nhắc việc" and "Xoá nhắc việc".
- **8c:** after the first save on this device, "Bật thông báo trên máy này?" leads to the OS prompt. It is asked once per install.
- **8d:** after that, while this device holds a notification it cannot show: "Đã lưu nhắc việc. Chưa bật thông báo trên máy này." + "Cài đặt", which opens the app's notification settings.
- **8e detail:**
  - Ngày giờ, Múi giờ, Báo trước, Thông báo ("iPhone của Mai"; elsewhere tap to move it here), Người tạo;
  - "Đánh dấu đã xong" / "Đánh dấu chưa xong", "Sửa nhắc việc".
- **ReminderPeek on Today:** "Nhắc việc · 3", the next reminder, and "Thứ Bảy 08:30 · còn 2 ngày". When there are none, a parent gets "Tạo nhắc việc". Relatives do not see it.
- **Notifications:**
  - `ReminderNotificationSync` wraps the app and keeps the OS's pending notifications equal to the open reminders this install holds, across every baby. It resyncs on start, on every change, after permission is granted and when returning from Settings.
  - Signing out cancels them all.
  - Tapping a notification switches to that baby and opens 8e.
- **Platform:**
  - flutter_local_notifications 22.3.1 and device_info_plus 13.2.0 (connectivity_plus held at 7.3.1 because of a Linux-only dbus conflict);
  - Android desugaring and boot receivers;
  - the iOS foreground notification delegate.

### Shared component change

- Detail rows now keep a short label at its natural width and give the value the rest. This fixes "Date of birth" wrapping in English on 11 and 14f, and long values on 8e.
