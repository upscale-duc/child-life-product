# S6 — History and statistics (in progress)

**Status:** History (20 – 20f, 12d) and the feeding and sleep statistics (20g, 20g2, 20h, plus the 30-day and sleep "Hôm nay" variants) are built. They read the same records that Today writes. No backend change was needed: the range query uses the existing `(deleted, occurredAt)` index, and the calendar dots read the `days/{date}` summaries that `onCareRecordWrite` already keeps. Nothing new was deployed.

**Not done:**

- The 1,000-row scroll budget from the plan is not measured yet. History renders one day at a time, and statistics read at most 31 days (capped at 3,000 rows), so no screen lists 1,000 rows.
- Diaper and pumping statistics are intentionally absent (G-063).
- Engineering-supplied copy waits for design review:
  - empty states for the other filters and for an all-empty day (G-064);
  - the 30-day and sleep "Hôm nay" views (G-066, D-028);

Checklist rows are ◐, never ☑.

## Decisions (recorded in the business map)

- **D-027** (Q-026): a sleep that crosses midnight shows its full span with the real start ("21:42 hôm qua – 06:30"). Day totals are clipped at midnight. "Giấc dài nhất" is the whole sleep, counted on the day it ended. The rhythm rows still split at midnight.
- **D-028** (Q-031): "30 ngày" and the sleep "Hôm nay" view reuse the 7-day cards: 30 rows or bars, or one row for today.

## Built

### Shared logic

- `care_calc.dart` is plain Dart with no Flutter or Firebase imports:
  - the day timeline by period: Sáng 05–11 · Trưa 11–13 · Chiều 13–18 · Tối 18–22 · Đêm 22–05;
  - per-type day cards: feeds with the last feed and the mean gap, sleep, diapers, pumping;
  - feeding and sleep statistics for Hôm nay, 7 days and 30 days.
- The Dart day maths is checked against the TypeScript `summarizeDay` with shared vectors (`packages/contracts/test/vectors/care-day.json`), so the app and the server count a day the same way, including midnight and time zones.

### App

- **20 History** (from Today "Lịch sử"):
  - day strip back to the birth date;
  - filters: Tất cả · Bú · Hút sữa · Ngủ · Tã · Tăng trưởng;
  - a summary card per filter, and the timeline by period with spans versus events;
  - pending pills, and running timers at the top of today;
  - tapping an item opens record detail (21).
- **Filters 20a / 20b / 20c:**
  - feed card with "Xem thống kê bú";
  - sleep card with "Đang thức" and "Xem thống kê ngủ";
  - diaper card where "Cả hai" counts as both.
- **20d Growth filter:** the latest measurement with its delta, a weight / length line chart and the measurement list. Growth's "Xem tất cả" opens History on this filter.
- **20e Empty day:** back-logging opens the sheet for that filter at the current time of day on the chosen date, in the baby's zone. Relatives see no back-log button.
- **20f Date picker:**
  - a month calendar with care-colour dots from the day summaries;
  - days before the birth date and after today are disabled;
  - "Về hôm nay".
- **12d:** caregivers see "Bạn sửa được bản ghi của mình. Bản ghi của ba mẹ chỉ xem." (D-022).
- **20g / 20g2 feeding statistics:**
  - Hôm nay, 7 ngày and 30 ngày; the screen remembers the last choice;
  - KPIs: ml, feeds, average gap;
  - day / night split by ml, with the insight on last night's longest gap;
  - an hourly average chart with the peak hour called out, or for Hôm nay one bar per bottle with breastfeeds as dots, shading the rest of the day as "Chưa tới";
  - breastfeed sides and the next-side hint.
- **20h sleep statistics:**
  - KPIs, the day / night split and the trend of the longest night sleep;
  - rhythm rows (one per day, split at midnight) with the current-time marker;
  - longest sleep per day.
