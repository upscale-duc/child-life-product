# S13 — External sharing: cards, links, recipient web (in progress)

**Status:** saved stories can leave the family as a photo card or a private link:

- the share sheet (14h);
- the cards (14i, 14j, 14n);
- the link options (14k);
- the recipient page with every state (14l, G-052);
- the manager under Tôi (14m).

These work end to end on Android against the local emulators. The backend is deployed to `child-life-dev`: Functions, Firestore Rules and indexes. The landing hosting serves `/s/**` from the `shareView` function. On dev, an unknown link already returns the calm "Liên kết không còn dùng được" page.

**Not done in S13:**

- **Universal / App Links.** The recipient's "Tải app" goes to the landing site. Opening a link in the app needs the Apple team (AASA).
- **Card sharing in tests.** The OS share sheet cannot be driven from a test. The card image (1080 × 1350 from the on-screen preview) and the "Thiệp ảnh đã lưu" record are covered by goldens and server tests, not by the E2E.
- **Desktop layout of the recipient page.** It is mobile-first and centred on wide screens.
- **iOS end-to-end run** is pending, as in S10–S12.

Checklist rows are ◐, never ☑.

## Decisions

**D-042** adopts P-095 and P-096 and fills the 14k–14m gaps:

- **Creating a link:**
  - A link is created on "Sao chép & chia sẻ", so no unused links are left behind.
  - Its options are fixed after creation.
  - The token is 16 random characters, followed by a nickname slug.
- **What the page shows** — a projection at view time:
  - the title and paragraphs;
  - only photos the family can see that still exist;
  - the nickname, the age and the sharer's given name;
  - the birth date only when "Ẩn tên thật & ngày sinh" is off.
- **Passcode:** shown once; 5 wrong tries lock an address for 15 minutes.
- **Page states:** revoked and unknown links show the same page; expired links say so.
- **Images:** served by the function after the same checks. Downloads only when allowed, and the copy never promises screenshot protection (R-166).
- **Cards** use only family-visible photos and are listed as irrevocable (R-162).
- **14m:** "Thu hồi" asks first.

## Built

### Backend (deployed to dev)

- **Contract:** `packages/contracts/src/links.ts` covers:
  - expiries 24 h / 7 d / 30 d (R-163);
  - passcode limits;
  - token, create, revoke and card requests;
  - `slugOf` ("Bé Na" → `be-na`);
  - `ageLabel` ("3 tuần tuổi").
- **Callables** (`firebase/functions/src/links.ts`):
  - `createStoryLink`: parents only, saved stories only. It stores the passcode hashed with a per-link salt and returns it once. It writes the 14m row (`users/{uid}/sharedOut`) and an audit event.
  - `revokeStoryLink`: the creator only.
  - `recordStoryCard`.
  - `expireStoryLinks` runs hourly.
- **`shareView`** (`/s/{token}[-slug]`, `/s/{token}/m/{mediaId}[/dl]`):
  - the live projection (P-095);
  - the passcode form with a per-link HMAC cookie, carried in Hosting's `__session` cookie;
  - a lock of 5 tries per 15 minutes per address (P-096);
  - one view counted per visitor, mirrored to 14m;
  - expired (410) and gone (404) pages;
  - `noindex`, no-store, a strict CSP and no referrer;
  - images streamed from Storage after the same checks; originals only with "Cho tải ảnh về".
- **Rules:**
  - `users/{uid}/sharedOut` is readable by its owner only.
  - `shareLinks` stays server-only (deny by default).
