# S9 — Memories core and upload (in progress)

**Status:** Kỷ niệm works end to end on iOS and Android against the dev backend code on the local emulators:

- the Nhật ký timeline by week of life and the Ảnh grid by month;
- adding a note, or photos and videos from the library or Files;
- the upload queue and pill;
- server processing;
- the viewer, selection actions and delete.

The backend is deployed to `child-life-dev`: Functions, Firestore Rules and indexes, Storage Rules (cross-service).

**Why S9 before S8:** S8 (widgets, lock screen, Live Activity) needs spike SP-1 on real devices, an Apple team (App Groups, keychain sharing) and APNs for push-to-start. None of that can be verified here, so S8 waits. The plan runs memories (S9–S13) as a parallel branch.

**Not done in S9:**

- **10a3 in-app burst camera:** "Chụp ảnh hoặc quay video" opens the system camera for one photo meanwhile.
- **10k share extension and 10j upload Live Activity:** they need the same Apple-team native work as S8.
- **Background transfer that survives an app kill (SP-2):** the queue is saved on the device and restarts unfinished items on the next launch (ADR-004 fallback).
- **Video processing (SP-3):** videos are stored as recorded with a device-made poster. Since 2026-10-19 a job adds a streaming rendition (`display.mp4`, H.264 ≤ 1080p30, fast start, metadata dropped — P-170) that the app plays; the original keeps its own metadata (which may carry a location).
- **Waiting on S10:**
  - Thương, Yêu thích, albums (10b3 Album, 10h5) and "Đặt làm ảnh bìa";
  - "Đã xoá gần đây" UI (G-022). Restore exists as the undo of a delete.
  - delivery of family notices to devices (they are recorded server-side).
- **10g "Có Teddy":** the OS does not expose face groups to apps, so the filter is absent (engineering gap).

Checklist rows are ◐, never ☑.

## Decisions

- **D-033** (Q-019, adopting P-030): the batch notice does not wait for videos held for Wi-Fi. The family hears once, when every item is done, or when at least one item is ready and the rest only wait for Wi-Fi on the uploader's device.
- **ADR-004** (interim answers for SP-2 and SP-3, plus serving):
  - reads go through Storage Rules (cross-service, same audience checks as Firestore), not signed URLs;
  - photos are JPEG from the device and re-encoded by `sharp` without metadata (EXIF and GPS dropped, P-033);
  - videos are stored as recorded;
  - the device queue is persisted.

## Built

### Backend (deployed to dev)

- **Contract:** `packages/contracts/src/memories.ts` holds the compose request and items (≤ 30, R-102), batch actions, effective audience (D-002, P-035), the batch-notice rule and content sniffing.
- **`createMemorySession`:**
  - creates the memory, its items and the batch;
  - reserves quota per item in a transaction (P-031);
  - items that do not fit stay on the device (`waitingQuota`, R-121);
  - retries with the same ids are idempotent.
- **`onMediaUploaded`** (Storage finalize):
  - checks the real content from its first bytes;
  - photos become `original` / `display` / `thumb` JPEGs without metadata;
  - videos move as they are, and their poster becomes `poster` / `thumb`;
  - commits the quota, or releases it for unreadable files.
- **`onMemoryMediaWrite`:**
  - keeps the memory's counts, cover and publish state (visible from its first ready item, P-030);
  - sends the batch's single generic notice once (R-120). The notice goes to the other parents and to relatives, never to caregivers.
- **`updateMemoryMedia`:**
  - "Ai thấy", including 10o with one notice;
  - "Đổi ngày", keeping the time of day in the baby's zone;
  - "Gắn mốc";
  - delete into "Đã xoá gần đây", and restore within 30 days.
- **Jobs and cancel:**
  - `cancelMemoryUploads` and `reserveMemoryUploads`;
  - `purgeMemoryBin` (30 days: bytes and quota released, P-034);
  - `releaseStaleMemoryUploads` (24 h).
- **Rules:**
  - Cùng nuôi read everything.
  - Relatives read only published Gia đình memories and ready Gia đình items.
  - Caregivers never read anything in Kỷ niệm.
  - Only Functions write memories and items. The uploader may only mark "waiting for Wi-Fi".
  - Family milestones (R-105).
  - Storage: the uploader fills only the reserved slot, within its size and type. Reads follow the same audience.

### App

- **10a Nhật ký:**
  - week-of-life groups ("Tuần thứ 3 · 1 – 7 tháng 10 · 6 ảnh, 3 video");
  - entries for 1 photo, 1 video, 2 photos, a note, or a grid with "+N";
  - author, "Hôm nay · 9:20", audience icon, milestone chip;
  - UploadPill: uploading, paused, failed, Wi-Fi wait, quota wait, done "Đã báo cho …".
- **10b Ảnh:**
  - month sections with counts and a date scrubber;
  - Chọn / Xong, "Chọn cả tháng", long-press to select;
  - layouts: small grid, large grid, list.
- **10a2:** taken-today strip (when the library is already shared), camera, library, Files, note.
- **10g picker:** Gần đây / Video / Album, at most 30, the tray "7 mục · 6 ảnh, 1 video · Tối đa 30 mục/lần", and limited access with "Chọn thêm".
- **10h compose:**
  - caption;
  - Ngày (10h2: by capture date, or one date between birth and today);
  - Mốc (10h3: age-based suggestions plus family milestones; 10h4: create and tag);
  - Ai thấy (Gia đình by default, D-001), "Báo cho gia đình", "Tải lên N mục".
  - Notes use the same sheet.
- **10i queue:**
  - summary, Tạm dừng / Tiếp tục, error counts;
  - rows: progress, Wi-Fi wait with size, "Mất kết nối" with Thử lại, unreadable with remove;
  - "Video chỉ tải qua Wi-Fi" (on by default), "Thử lại tất cả (N)".
- **10b3 actions:** Ai thấy (→ 10o when widening), Tải về (to Photos), Gửi đi (share sheet), Gắn mốc, Đổi ngày.
- **10b4:** delete for the whole family, with undo.
- **10b5:** go to month; months without media are disabled.
- **10c:** group, layout, show, audience; "Xem như người thân" with a banner and Thoát.
- **10f viewer:**
  - swipe, audience pill, milestone, caption, "Thứ Hai, 5 thg 10 · Mẹ đăng";
  - Tải về and Chia sẻ;
  - videos play after a Rules-checked download.
- **Relatives:** see only what is shared, with no "+" and no select. Caregivers have no Kỷ niệm tab (12a).
