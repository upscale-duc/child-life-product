## Built

### Contracts (`packages/contracts`)

- **`staff.ts`.** Roles are sets of levels per portal function:
  - 13 functions and 6 levels;
  - eight built-in roles; Owner, Admin and Kiểm thử are locked, and `lead` is read as Admin;
  - ceilings for Hỗ trợ;
  - `staffCan(levels, action)`;
  - schemas for invitations, member actions, roles, families and analytics.
- **`reference.ts`.**
  - The four datasets with their row schemas.
  - Version 1 is the copy the app shipped: `who-reference.g.ts` is generated from the app's WHO LMS tables by `scripts/who-reference.mjs`, bottle v1 is P-126, vaccines v1 is P-133.
  - Diff, and a CSV parser with line-numbered errors.

### Functions (`firebase/functions/src`)

- **`staff-access.ts`.**
  - Every staff callable resolves the caller from `staffMembers` and `staffRoles`, so a pause, removal or role edit applies on the next call.
  - Callables: `staffMe`, `staffTeam`, `staffInvite` (7-day invitations, `staffInvite` mail), `staffUpdateMember` (roles, access window, pause / resume / remove, revoke invitation; the last Owner and yourself are protected), `staffSaveRole` (never more than the editor has; people management stays with Owner / Admin).
  - `staffAcceptInvite` on the first email-link sign-in.
  - `expireStaffAccessJob`, hourly.
- **`staff.ts`.**
  - Level checks on every A02–A12 callable; P-120 is now "Mở rộng dung lượng: Duyệt".
  - Re-running a failed deletion takes two people: `staffRerunJob` asks, and `staffApproveRerun` approves and runs, by a different person with "Duyệt" (P-139).
  - Audit role names; A02 counts heartbeat days as activity (P-137).
- **`staff-families.ts`.**
  - The `familyDirectory` read model, kept by triggers on families, members and invites, nightly, and on every detail open.
  - `staffFamilies`: KPIs, activity / platform / storage filters, pages of 25, search by code or exact email (never echoed).
  - `staffFamily`: counts only; no family or baby name, age, custom label, device name or full email; logged as `family.view`.
  - `staffOpenTransfer` / `staffCancelTransfer`, and the owner's `answerStaffOwnershipRequest` (agreeing makes the usual owner offer).
  - `expireFamilyTransfersJob`.
- **`analytics.ts`.**
  - `recordInstallSource` (Android referrer).
  - `activityOnDevice`: a heartbeat day marks the person's families active.
  - `rollupDaily` at 00:30 Vietnam time: backfills up to 30 missing days into `statsDaily`, then refreshes every directory row. Each day holds DAU / WAU / MAU, D30, new families by channel and platform, feature counts, week-feature use, storage, versions and cohorts.
  - `staffAnalytics` / `staffExportAnalytics` (export is logged).
- **`reference-data.ts`.**
  - `staffReference`, `staffReferenceDraft` (validated: schema, unique rows, joined bands, ±30 % on WHO rows), and `staffReferenceAct` (submit, approve by someone else, reject, discard, rollback).
  - Approval publishes to `publicConfig/reference_*`; the first release keeps v1 in the history.
- **Mail templates** `staffInvite` and `ownershipStaffRequest` (vi / en).
- **Rules.**
  - `publicConfig/reference_*` are readable.
  - Device rows accept `lastActiveAt` (server time only), `osVersion` and a coarse `model`.
  - Everything new stays callable-only.
- **Indexes.** The directory filters, transfers, staff expiry, member lookups, and the collection-group date fields used by the rollup.

### Portal (`apps/portal`)

- **Shell.**
  - The v1.0 sidebar, with sections (VẬN HÀNH, HỆ THỐNG, PHÁT TRIỂN); each member sees only what their levels open. Section headings follow the first visible item.
  - Nested routes (`/families/F-XXXX`, `/staff/roles/<id>`, `/analytics/features`, `/reference/<table>`).
  - `identify()` takes the levels from `staffMe` and accepts an invitation on the first sign-in.
- **New screens.**
  - Nhân sự & phân quyền: members, the roles matrix, the role editor with ceilings, invitations, the invite dialog.

  - Dữ liệu tham chiếu: WHO tables, bottle editor, vaccine schedule and editor, CSV import with row errors and diff, draft / submit / approve / reject / rollback, history.
- **Existing screens.**
  - Yêu cầu dữ liệu shows "Chờ duyệt · đề nghị bởi …" and lets a different approver approve.
  - A12 has the new categories.
  - Strings vi / en.

### Mobile (`apps/mobile`, P-136 / P-137 / P-138)

## Portal UI review (2026-10-11, deployed to dev)

- **Sidebar:** fixed in place; the menu scrolls inside it while the brand and the user card stay put, and the open page scrolls into view. At 860 px and below a top bar opens it as a drawer.
- **Tables:** 17 tables scroll sideways inside their own frame (`TableScroll`), with a minimum width per table.
- **Bar lists** (`HBars`) are sized by their panel through a container query; in a narrow column the bar moves under the label. This fixes the A13 "Nguồn gia đình mới" values that spilled out of the card.
- **Dialogs and the case sheet:** they lock the page scroll, the dialog buttons stay at the bottom, and Escape closes only the top layer (`useEscape`).
- **Dữ liệu tham chiếu:** switching tables drew the bottle / vaccine editor with the previous table's rows, which left a blank page. The screen now waits for the table that was asked for (`ReferenceFor`).
- **Sign-in:** the email link and "Quên mật khẩu" go through `sendAuthEmail` (D-054), falling back to Firebase's own mail on `unavailable`.
- **Checks:** no page overflow at 1440, 1280×720, 1024×768, 768×1024 or 390×844.

## Cập nhật ứng dụng for small desktops (owner, 2026-10-13)

- **Tabs and status:**
  - Three tabs (Phiên bản / Có gì mới / Trang giới thiệu) instead of one long form.
  - "Đang áp dụng" is one line, and the history opens in a sheet.
- **Publishing:** "Hoàn tác / Xuất bản…" is pinned at the bottom.
  - Wrong values are flagged at the field right away. Empty ones are flagged after a publish attempt, which also opens the tab and focuses the first one.
  - Tabs show their problem counts.
  - The confirmation shows a before → after table.
- **Phiên bản:** iOS and Android are one table (a stacked layout below 520 px) with one shared legend.
- **Lists:** highlights and pages open one at a time, with ↑ ↓ ✕ on the row.
- **Preview:**
  - It sits beside the form when the content area is at least 900 px (a CSS container query); otherwise "Xem trước" opens it in a sheet.
  - The phone scales to the window height (0.4–0.72), so it is whole at 1280 × 720.
  - A screen picker sits next to the palette, and the language and platform toggles share one row.

## Góp ý in the portal (P-145, D-065, 2026-10-13)

- **Server** (`staff.ts`, `analytics.ts`):
  - Ticket rows carry `feedbackType` and `contactable`; the detail carries `hasScreenshot`.
  - A reply to a Góp ý sent without "Cho phép liên hệ lại" is refused (`no-contact`).
  - `staffTicketScreenshot` (support view) returns the image inline as a data URL, never a link. Only paths under `feedback/` are accepted, the limit is 8 MB, and each view is logged as `support.screenshot`.
  - Phân tích › Tính năng adds the app's `appEventsDaily` counters for the range: Góp ý by type and review-prompt eligible / shown, all platforms. They are also in the CSV.
- **Portal** (Hỗ trợ):
  - "Góp ý · Báo lỗi" in the list and the detail, a Loại filter (Tất cả / Hỗ trợ / Góp ý), and "Không liên hệ lại" in place of the email.
  - The no-contact note hides the reply box and the Wi-Fi macro.
  - "Xem ảnh chụp màn hình" loads the image on request.
  - Phân tích has the "Góp ý & đánh giá" panel.
- **Tests:**
  - `staff.integration` covers type, no-contact, the screenshot, its audit entry and the refusals.
  - `analytics.integration` covers the counters in range.
  - Portal tests cover the list, the detail and the panel.
