## Decisions

**D-041** adopts P-090–P-092 and resolves Q-036:

- **Weekly suggestion:**
  - needs ≥ 5 new items, at most 3 per baby per week;
  - text only;
  - ≤ 9 photos attached by the server;
  - output checked against the sources, or a template draft instead.
- **Drafts:** private to their writer.
- **Saved stories:**
  - any Cùng nuôi may edit a saved story;
  - one notice on the first Gia đình save, never again;
  - photos shown in a Gia đình story are seen by the family (D-002);
  - deleting a story keeps its photos.

## Built

### Backend (deployed to dev)

- **Contract:** `packages/contracts/src/stories.ts` holds:
  - the blocks: paragraph (with an `ai` flag), photos (1–4, captions), milestone, quote;
  - the save, delete, assist and suggest requests;
  - the AI draft shape;
  - `guardAiText`: numbers and capitalised names must come from the sources, the nickname or relation labels; no links (P-092, R-136).
- **AI adapter** (`firebase/functions/src/ai.ts`):
  - OpenAI chat completions with a JSON schema, `store: false`, a 30 s timeout;
  - the model is set by `OPENAI_MODEL`;
  - no photos, nickname only (R-196);
  - the template provider writes plain drafts from the sources.
- **Callables** (`firebase/functions/src/stories.ts`):
  - `saveFamilyStory`:
    - re-validates the draft and checks its photos exist;
    - keeps `familyStoryIds` on media, so the effective audience follows D-002 (`albums.ts` `recomputeEffective`);
    - returns how many parents-only photos become visible;
    - sends one `story` notice on the first Gia đình save (P-091).
  - `deleteFamilyStory` removes the story's family photo links and loves (G-050).
  - `storyParagraphAssist`: rewrite, shorter, warmer, suggest; guarded; 60 per person per day.
  - `requestStorySuggestion` and `weeklyStorySuggestions` (Sunday 19:00 Vietnam time):
    - ≥ 5 items, ≤ 3 per week, the flag;
    - milestone photos first;
    - a template fallback when the model fails or invents (G-051).
- **Rules:**
  - Parents create and edit their drafts and can take a suggestion.
  - A saved story's content can be edited, but its status and audience go through Functions.
  - Drafts and suggestions can be discarded; saved stories cannot be deleted directly.
  - Relatives read saved Gia đình stories; caregivers never.
  - Loves only on saved Gia đình stories.
  - Nothing is written while the profile is deleting.
