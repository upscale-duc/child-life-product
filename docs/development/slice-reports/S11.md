# S11 — Me, storage, profiles, account, deletion (in progress)

**Status:** the Tôi tab and its pages work in the app against the dev backend code on the local emulators. This covers storage with requests, baby profiles (view, edit, delete), your profile, account and devices, ownership transfer, data export, notification settings and help.

The backend is deployed to `child-life-dev`:

- Functions;
- Firestore Rules and indexes;
- Storage Rules.

All 11 new callables are invokable by app users.

**Not done in S11:**

- **Staff side of storage requests and help tickets.** Granting, declining and replying belong to the operations portal (S14). Requests and tickets are recorded and announced by email to `SUPPORT_INBOX`. That address is a placeholder until the owner names the real inbox. Email delivery waits on the SES keys.
- **Push on devices.** As in S10, iOS needs APNs and an Apple team. Notices are recorded and filtered by the notification settings.
- **User profile photo** (13m "Đổi ảnh"). People are shown by their initial (D-040, G-078).
- **Changing the email address.** "Đổi mật khẩu" sends a reset email (G-059).
- **"Đã chia sẻ ra ngoài"** (14m link manager) comes with S13.
- **Storage reconciliation.** It is done against the stored items' recorded sizes. A Storage-bucket scan is not done.
- **iOS end-to-end run.** On this simulator, `flutter test` stalls waiting for the Dart VM service log line, although the app starts under `flutter run`. Android runs pass, and iOS is to be re-run on a fresh simulator.

Checklist rows are ◐, never ☑.

## Decisions

These adopt the business-map proposals:

- **D-037** (Q-016) — transfer and account deletion:
  - The owner offers the whole family to an active Cùng nuôi of every baby, who accepts in the app.
  - An owner cannot delete their account.
  - A deleted account's care records, memories and messages stay, shown as "Người dùng đã xoá".
- **D-038** (Q-037) — undrawn entry points:
  - "Xuất dữ liệu" lives in 13n.
  - "Trợ giúp & liên hệ" is an in-app form, with diagnostics only with consent.
  - A "đang được xoá" banner shows while writes are locked during a profile deletion.
- **D-039** — devices:
  - The device list has no city.
  - Remote sign-out flags the device's row; that install signs itself out when it connects.
  - Refresh tokens are not revoked.
- **D-040** — people are shown by their initial in V1.
- **P-003** is applied: any Cùng nuôi of the family sees storage and can request more (G-040).

## Built

### Backend (deployed to dev)

- **Contract:** `packages/contracts/src/account.ts` holds:
  - storage states (80 % / 100 %) and requests (+10 / +20 GB, D-010);
  - baby edit and delete, profile, ownership, devices;
  - notification preferences, export, help tickets and diagnostics.
- **Storage** (`firebase/functions/src/account.ts`, `memories.ts`):
  - every item change keeps `families/{f}.usage.{baby}` (photos, videos and their bytes) and `binBytes` (G-041);
  - a daily `reconcileStorageUsage` rebuilds the breakdown from the items;
  - `getFamilyStorage` returns totals, the breakdown by baby and by type, an estimate for "Câu chuyện & ghi chú", the 20 largest videos (G-058) and an open request.
- **Storage notices:** `maybeStorageNotice` sends one generic notice per state change at 80 % and 100 % to the family's parents.
- **Storage requests:** `requestMoreStorage` keeps one open request per family. The requester gets an acknowledgement and staff get a notice. Neither email contains the message text (P-124).
- **Baby profile:**
  - `updateBabyProfileInfo` is for Cùng nuôi; the date of birth must be in range in the baby's zone.
  - `deleteBabyProfile` is owner-only and checks the typed nickname. It locks writes (baby `state: deleting`) and queues a job.
- **Deletion jobs** (`runDeletion` trigger, `resumeDeletions` every 15 minutes, P-110):
  - Baby steps:
    - lock;
    - Storage prefix;
    - every subcollection, invites, exports and reservations;
    - family (quota rebuilt, owner count);
    - verify (no collections, objects or links left);
    - finish.
  - A failure records the failed step and the job resumes there later. The profile document goes only at the end, so "đã xoá" is never shown early (flow F).
  - Account steps: memberships; authored content marked "Người dùng đã xoá"; loves and favourites removed; personal data; sign-in; confirmation email.
- **Profile and ownership:**
  - `updateMyProfileInfo` changes the display name on every membership and the relation per baby.
  - `offerFamilyOwnership` and `answerFamilyOwnership` handle accept, decline and cancel. Accepting moves the family, its babies, the owner flags and the counts.
  - `deleteMyAccount` refuses owners.
- **Devices:** `signOutMyDevice` flags another install and drops its push token. Notices respect each account's "Thông báo" settings.
- **Exports** (`requestDataExport` and `buildDataExport`, R-187, P-123):
  - care logs as CSV and JSON for Cùng nuôi; photos and videos as a ZIP for the owner only;
  - files go to `exports/{uid}/{job}/`;
  - Storage Rules re-check the membership and ownership at download, until the 24-hour expiry;
  - `expireDataExports` removes expired files.
- **Help:** `createHelpTicket` creates numbered tickets (`CL-1001`, …) with optional diagnostics (no child content).
- **Rules:**
  - Writes to a baby stop while it is deleting, in Firestore and Storage.
  - Device rows: the install may update its own row but never set or clear `signedOutAt`.
  - Notification settings belong to their owner.
  - Deletion jobs and exports are readable by their requester only.
