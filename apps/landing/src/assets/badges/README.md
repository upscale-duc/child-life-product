# Store badges

Official artwork, unchanged except for trimming the transparent margin of the Google Play PNGs.
Downloaded 2026-10-21 with the owner's approval.

- `app-store-{vi,en}.svg` — Apple "Download on the App Store", black, vi-VN / en-US
  (toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com). Follow Apple's App Store marketing guidelines.
- `google-play-{vi,en}.png` — Google "Get it on Google Play", vi / en
  (play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges). Follow Google Play's badge guidelines.

Show both at the same height. Before launch the badges are pictures, not links (owner,
2026-10-19); `site.ts` `storeLinks` turns them into links to the listings.
