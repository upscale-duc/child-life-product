# Landing photos — sources and licences

Real photographs for the image slots of the landing (`docs/discovery/figma-extract/28-landing-v5b-home.md` §7), chosen 2026-10-13 and approved by the owner. Downloaded at 1600 px from the source, then resized and recompressed (JPEG q72) with `sips`; `toddler-blocks.jpg` is mirrored and `first-school-day.jpg` is a portrait crop.

**The first year is one baby, "Teddy".** Every `teddy-*.jpg` comes from Minnie Zhou's Unsplash album (@marslady), which follows one boy from birth to about one year (uploads Dec 2018 – Dec 2019). It is very probably the same child throughout; this is not confirmed. These photos replace Figma's 220 px `Illustration/Photo` library photos everywhere (`photos` in `components/primitives.tsx`) and fill the first-year slots in `components/scenes.ts`. The later stages (1–3, 3–6, 6–12 years) are other children.

Both licences allow free commercial use, modification and no attribution; they do **not** include model releases and forbid implying that the people shown endorse Child Life. Do not use these photos in ads that suggest an endorsement, and replace them with commissioned photos if that ever matters.
